Our Story

Cooked at Home, Shared with Love

The Indian Plate started in our own kitchen, cooking the recipes passed down through generations of our family. What began as meals for friends and neighbours has grown into a small home-based kitchen serving our local community around Rugby.

We only use fresh ingredients, whole spices ground in-house, and slow-cooking methods that take time — because that's how proper Indian food is meant to be made. Nothing is mass-produced and nothing is frozen.

Every order is prepared fresh for collection or local delivery, the same way we'd cook for our own family.
